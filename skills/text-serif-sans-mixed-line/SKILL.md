# text-serif-sans-mixed-line

> Library technique (category: `text`). Shared rules: [CONTRACT](../../../library/CONTRACT.md). > Execution part only. Analysis notes (reference measurements, history): [ANALYSIS.md](ANALYSIS.md) - open only when a decision needs them.

## Observable behavior
On full-canvas proof scenes (cream background, phone mock-up) the spoken sentence is set as a short stacked headline that **alternates typefaces**: connector words in a small light *italic serif*, the key words in a **bold black grotesque sans**, sometimes larger ("**exactly**", "**competitors**", "**success**"). Words appear one by one in speech order, each fading from light grey to its final colour in ~3–4 frames; the lines are left-aligned or centred above/next to the mock-up.

## Intended effect
Looks editorial and premium; the bold words carry the message at a glance while the serif words keep the sentence natural.

## Defining characteristics
- Two contrasting faces in the same line: small italic serif + bold heavy sans [Fixed]
- Only the key words (1–2 per line) are sans/bold; they may be ~1.5–2× bigger [Rule]
- Word-by-word, grey → black, on the spoken word [Rule]
- Stacked 2–4 short lines, placed near the visual it explains (above a phone, beside a close-up) [Rule]

## When to use
- Triggers: a sentence that explains what a shown product/screen does or proves.
- Good fit: cream/light canvas scenes, product or proof shots.
- Avoid when: busy footage behind the text.

## Anatomy over time
- Start: first word on its speech onset.
- Development: one word per spoken word; the bold word settles last within its line.
- Resolution: holds until the scene cut.

## Relationships
- Visuals: sits beside `graphic-phone-mockup-feed`; may be joined to it by a line from `graphic-hand-drawn-annotation`.
- Captions: the grey caption pill is not used on these full-canvas scenes; this line is the caption.
- Sound effects: none observed on the words.

## Adaptation
- Fonts: brand serif italic (or a classic italic serif) + brand heavy sans; colour near-black, bold word may use the brand accent.
- Scaling: serif words ≈ 2–2.5 % of frame height, bold words ≈ 3.5–5 %.

## Key numbers
- Sentence build at 00:30.680–00:31.930: "it shows you the" → "best" → "performing" → "videos in your niche", word by word with speech, grey → black ~3–4 frames (observed; table in ANALYSIS.md).
- "exactly" at 00:32.940: bold sans ≈ 2× the italic words, grey → black over ~4 frames (observed; table in ANALYSIS.md).

## Success criteria
- At a glance the bold words alone carry the meaning.
- Each word lands on its spoken word.

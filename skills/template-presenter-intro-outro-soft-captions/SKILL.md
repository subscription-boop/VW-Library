## Intention and style
A light, likeable presenter talking straight to camera on a plain neutral background: soft, modern phrase captions, one big playful hook word, and two or three colourful accents. Voice with a few small sound effects, no music.

## Techniques and roles
| Technique | Role |
| :- | :- |
| `zoom-push-in-overshoot` | first frame: quick push-in with a soft landing |
| `title-word-behind-subject-rise` | the greeting/hook word, behind the head |
| `text-phrase-build-blur-rise` | the caption track throughout |
| `graphic-emoji-icon-under-caption` | a named place/brand/object |
| `text-keyword-blur-backdrop` | the topic phrase ("FIRST DAY") |
| `text-neon-glow-word` | the goal/payoff word |
| `transition-negative-flash` + `transition-light-leak-flash` | right after the payoff word, hiding a jump cut |
| `transition-whip-pan` | exit into the embedded piece (when the video continues with another section, e.g. the podcast template) |
| `sfx-noise-burst-flash` | short static/air burst on the negative/light-leak flash |
| `sfx-whoosh-soft-transition` | soft low whoosh on the whip out into the embedded piece |

## Sequencing
- Intro (≈ 8–10 s): push-in + hook word → name/age/origin phrases (icon on the origin) → topic keyword backdrop → goal word in neon → negative/light-leak flash → 1–2 plain phrases → whip out.
- Outro (≈ 3–5 s): hard cut back, plain phrase captions only (no accents), call for feedback/comment.

## Rhythm
- Phrase every 0.5–1.5 s; one accent graphic about every 2 s in the intro; outro calm.

## Audio
- Voice plus two effects: `sfx-noise-burst-flash` on the flash (~1 dB under the voice peaks, in the pause between sentences) and `sfx-whoosh-soft-transition` on the whip out. Presenter captions are silent (no typing sounds). [Observed, src-003 sound analysis 2026-10-24]
- No music under the presenter. [Observed]

## Scaling
- Intro only (no embedded piece): end on the payoff word + flash, skip the whip.

## Success criteria
- Feels warm and personal; the accents land on the words they illustrate; the face is never covered.

# graphic-photo-card-flash-burst

> Library technique (category: `graphic`). Shared rules: [CONTRACT](../../../library/CONTRACT.md). To apply this to a video, follow [QUICK-CREATE](../../../library/QUICK-CREATE.md) (full rules: [EXECUTION](../../../library/EXECUTION.md)). The name is an identifier only: read this document for behavior.
> Execution part only. Analysis notes (reference measurements, registration history): [ANALYSIS.md](ANALYSIS.md) - open only when a decision needs them.

## Observable behavior
A wide B-roll shot of a house stays on screen. In its centre a tall rectangular photo card (~0.5 W × 0.7 H, no border, hard edges) appears and is replaced every 4 frames by the next one: leaf, pebbles, sea, pool tiles, curtain. Some cards are slightly rotated or start with a short motion smear. After the fifth card the burst ends with a whip transition to the next shot. The background shot keeps playing behind the cards.

## Intended effect
Shows many details (materials, textures, moments) in a split second; adds a stop-motion "flip-book" energy inside a montage.

## Defining characteristics
- Background shot continues; cards sit in the centre over it. [Fixed]
- One card at a time, hard swaps, ~4 frames each (~0.13 s). [Rule]
- 4–6 cards of related detail images. [Observed]

## When to use
- Triggers: a montage moment about quality/details/variety (materials, products, projects).
- Avoid when: the cards would need to be read (text) — they are on screen only ~0.13 s.

## Anatomy over time
- Start: ~0.5 s into a held background shot.
- Development: card swaps every 4 frames.
- Resolution: the burst ends on the next transition.
- Repetition: once per montage.

## Relationships
- Music: inside the beat montage; the burst spans about one beat.
- Sound effects: none observed beyond the music.
- Other techniques: part of `cut-beat-broll-montage` ([SKILL.md](../cut-beat-broll-montage/SKILL.md)).

## Adaptation
- Adaptable: number of cards, image content, card size (keep inside the safe area).
- Brand: none (photographic); a thin brand-colour border is acceptable if the brand uses frames.

## Key numbers
- All values are stated above; the per-source table is in ANALYSIS.md.

## Success criteria
- The cards read as a quick flip-book over a continuing shot; total burst ≤ ~0.7 s.

## Intention and style
A fast, clean "N tips" explainer for a seated talking head. The camera never moves digitally; the energy comes from big word-by-word type, full-screen flat-colour cards between sections, and one small motion graphic per tip that literally shows what the speaker is saying. Look: natural, slightly dark footage; flat red or light cards; heavy white type with one highlight colour.

## Relationships and dependencies
- Exactly one caption layer at a time: top captions by default; chest captions while the hook title is up (and optionally for one calmer section).
- No captions on full-screen cards or while a label box is on screen.
- Each tip gets one main demonstration graphic, chosen by what the tip is about. Demonstration graphics never stack on each other.
- A new label box or graphic usually starts on a jump cut at the same framing.

## Triggers and sequencing principles
- Opening (0–~5 s): `title-stacked-hook` from frame 1 with chest captions underneath; optional `graphic-level-meter` on a "from … to …" line; when the title leaves, switch to top captions.
- Series card right after the hook if the video is part of a series, then the first tip card.
- Body, per tip: tip card → talking head with captions → one demonstration graphic on the sentence that explains the point.
- Mid-video CTA: on "comment <keyword>", the keyword in the highlight colour; place it right after the most useful demonstration.
- Ending: the last tip may skip its card and run straight to the final line; end on speech, no outro card. [Observed]

## Rhythm and emphasis
- Captions change on every word; demonstration graphics change roughly every 1.5–4 s; cards last ~0.9–1.7 s.
- Different tips may use different framings of the same camera (tip 1 was ~8 % tighter in the reference). [Observed]
- No zooms, no transitions other than hard cuts. [Rule]

## Audio-visual coordination
- Continuous music bed under the voice; the voice always clearly on top.
- If a music hit falls within a few frames of a card cut or a graphic entrance, put the event on the hit. [Rule, from observation]
- No separate sound effects were found on graphics in the reference. [Uncertain]

## Scaling to other lengths and structures
- Shorter (≤30 s, 2 tips): keep the hook and the cards; drop the meter and the inset card unless the content needs them.
- Longer (5+ tips): one card per tip; vary demonstration graphics so neighbouring tips don't repeat the same one.
- Not suited to no-speech or music-only videos: the approach is driven by spoken tips.

## Template-specific adaptations and constraints
- Cards, highlight colour, caption font and label colours follow the selected brand; keep one accent colour across the whole video.
- Keep numbering style consistent across cards (the reference mixed a boxed and a red numeral). [Rule]

## Success criteria
- Every word is readable on mute.
- Every tip has a visual that shows the tip itself, not decoration.
- Section breaks are unmistakable.
- Nothing covers the speaker's face for more than a moment.

# zoom-cut-in-tight-pull-reveal

> Library technique (category: `zoom`). Shared rules: [CONTRACT](../../../library/CONTRACT.md). To apply this to a video, follow [QUICK-CREATE](../../../library/QUICK-CREATE.md) (full rules: [EXECUTION](../../../library/EXECUTION.md)). The name is an identifier only: read this document for behavior.
> Execution part only. Analysis notes (reference measurements, overlaps, history): [ANALYSIS.md](ANALYSIS.md) - open only when a decision needs them.

## Observable behavior
A hard cut brings a new location, but framed tight on the standing speaker (he fills the middle; the environment is cropped away). The tight framing **holds still for ~0.4–0.5 s** while he starts the sentence ("If you're looking…"). Then the picture pulls out in one smooth S-shaped move (slow start, fast middle, soft landing) in ~0.6 s to a wide shot (~1.7x wider) that shows the whole setting: bridge, water, dock. The wide framing then stays static. The first word of a stacked title lockup ("BIGGER") starts appearing in the open space as the pull-out lands.

## Intended effect
A reveal: the viewer first sees only the person, then the space opens up around him. It gives a section start scale and "production value" and creates room for a big title.

## Defining characteristics
- Starts on a cut, already very tight (~1.6–1.8x of the final wide). [Rule]
- A short still hold before moving (~0.4–0.5 s). [Rule]
- Pull-out only, S-curve (slow-fast-slow), no overshoot; ~0.6 s for ~1.7x. [Rule]
- Ends on a static wide shot that shows the location. [Fixed]
- The wide shot's empty area receives a title/graphic as the move ends. [Rule]

## When to use
- Triggers: a new section or location; a promise sentence ("If you're looking to create a bigger impact…").
- Good fit: wide, high-resolution footage with an interesting environment around the speaker.
- Avoid when: the source is not sharp enough to crop ~1.7x, or the environment adds nothing.

## Anatomy over time
- Start: cut lands tight; speech starts.
- Development: ~0.45 s hold, then the pull-out (~0.6 s, steepest about 0.2 s in).
- Resolution: static wide; title lockup builds in the space above the head.
- Repetition: once in the reference.

## Relationships
- Subject: zoom centre ≈ speaker's chest.
- Speech: the pull-out starts mid-sentence (after the first 2–3 words), the title word lands with its spoken word.
- Cuts: begins on a hard cut from a tighter walking/medium shot.
- Sound effects: none observed.
- Other techniques: `text-key-phrase-stacked-lockup` ([SKILL.md](../text-key-phrase-stacked-lockup/SKILL.md)) lands in the revealed space.

## Adaptation
- Adaptable: start tightness (~1.4–1.8x) by source resolution; hold length by the first words.
- Brand: none.

## Key numbers
- Still hold ~0.43 s; pull-out ×0.59 in ~0.6 s, steepest 00:06.07–00:06.14; cut lands at ~1.7x of the final wide (observed; table in ANALYSIS.md).
- The title word ("BIGGER") starts fading in right as the move settles (observed).

## Success criteria
- The viewer reads "tight → big reveal"; the hold before moving is visible.
- The move is one smooth S-curve, no bounce.
- The title appears in the newly revealed space, not on the face.

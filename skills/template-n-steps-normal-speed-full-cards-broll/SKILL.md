## Intention and style
Calm, premium "N steps" explainer at a normal talking pace. Small, elegant captions instead of big ones; emphasis comes from type (bold sans, italic serif), not motion. Proof first, then a pause card, then the steps, each broken up by warm, low-light cinematic B-roll that matches the words. Motion is subtle: soft pull-out zooms on section starts, one blur bridge, one flicker.

## Relationships and dependencies
- One caption track throughout (tiers 1–3), continuing over B-roll and above inset cards; on white cards it turns black.
- Step labels replace the caption for ~0.5–0.7 s at each step start.
- The whoosh belongs only to the set-up → first step cut; other cuts stay silent (no whoosh on every cut).
- Pull-out zooms and step labels often share the same cut.
- `text-step-label-serif` sits on the `cut-section-header-beat` shot.
- The kept emphasis pauses of `cut-tight-phrase-joins` are where tier-2/3 emphasis and `text-flicker-in` land.
- A running pull-out zoom continues across a same-framing jump cut.
- The grade applies to the speaker's footage only; B-roll, inset cards, white cards, captions and labels keep their own colours. [User]
- White cards carry a subtle photographic paper texture, a different paper each card (see `card-white-word-beat`); papers A–E are approved, no question needed (2026-11-01). [User]
- If the footage is bright/daylit, tell the user the grade only approximates the look (see the grade skill).

## Triggers and sequencing principles
- Hook (0–~2.5 s): `zoom-cut-ease-out` from frame 1; tier-3 serif result number; `graphic-ui-stat-popup` with the real stat.
- Set-up: `card-white-word-beat` ("Here's how you do it"), key phrase in serif ("3 steps."); `sfx-whoosh-main-start` ending on the cut into step 1.
- Each step: cut → `text-step-label-serif` (+ optional `zoom-cut-ease-out`) → captions with tiers → 1–2 B-roll inserts that literally match the words (writing, thinking, gym, phone…) → at most one demonstration device per step (inset card, blur bridge).
- Pivot inside a step: white card with the pivot word flickering in.
- Close: action word on a cut with `zoom-cut-ease-out`; final word in tier-3 serif with `text-flicker-in`, held to the end.

## Rhythm and emphasis
- Normal speech pace; captions change on every word.
- Rough cut first: `cut-tight-phrase-joins` on the voice, then shots per `cut-rhythm-phrase-shots` (average ~1.5 s, range ~0.5–4 s; B-roll chains 0.7–1.5 s per shot; white cards 0.5–1.7 s; header beats ~0.6 s; about one visible jump cut per 8–10 s).
- Tier 2 a few times per step; tier 3 about once per sentence at most.

## B-roll
- Cinematic, warm or low-key, shallow depth of field, the speaker himself in other moments (thinking, writing, training, on the phone).
- Each insert illustrates the words being said at that moment; captions continue on top.
- B-roll comes from the user's own footage or sources he approves; ask before downloading anything.

## Audio-visual coordination
- Voice always on top. One whoosh into the main content. Quiet low hits or a soft music bed may appear later [Uncertain in the reference]; ask the user before adding music.

## Scaling to other lengths and structures
- Shorter (2 steps, ≤20 s): keep the proof hook, one white card, step labels, one B-roll per step; drop the blur bridge.
- Longer (5+ steps): one B-roll insert per step; vary the demonstration device; keep only 2–3 pull-out zooms in total.
- No proof screenshot available: skip `graphic-ui-stat-popup`; never invent a number.

## Template-specific adaptations and constraints
- Fonts: a clean sans for tiers 1–2 and an elegant italic serif for tier 3 / step labels, from the selected brand when it has them.
- White cards may use the brand's light surface; keep them flat with nothing else on them.

## Success criteria
- Every claim has proof or a matching B-roll.
- Emphasis tiers are consistent and meaningful.
- Exactly one whoosh, on the entry into the main content.
- The video ends on the serif word.
